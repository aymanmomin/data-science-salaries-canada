## 🎯 Quick Insights

### Top 3 Provinces for Data Scientists:
1. **British Columbia:** $141k median (Vancouver tech hub)
2. **Ontario:** $129k median (Toronto AI corridor)
3. **Alberta:** $114k median (energy + tech sectors)

### For Your Co-op Search:
- **Entry-level:** ~$79-83k/year ($38-40/hour)
- **Median:** $114k in Alberta, competitive!
- **Senior potential:** Up to $229k in Alberta
- **Hot skills:** Python, SQL, ML frameworks (TensorFlow/PyTorch)
- **Target industries:** Tech, Finance, Energy (AB strength!), Healthcare
